About

Richard Visagie is a South African engineer and Embedded Systems Engineering master's student at the University of Freiburg, Germany, with a BEng in Mechatronic Engineering from Stellenbosch University.

Education

  • MSc Embedded Systems Engineering, University of Freiburg, Germany (since 2026)
  • BEng Mechatronic Engineering, Stellenbosch University, South Africa (2022 to 2025)

Awards

  • Chairperson's Prize, Department of Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering, Stellenbosch University (2025)
  • Leadership Merit Award, Stellenbosch University (2024)
  • Golden Key Award, Golden Key International Honour Society (2022)

Leadership

Member of the Student Representative Council of Stellenbosch University (2024), the highest elected student body at the university.
